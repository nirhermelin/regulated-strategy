The Next Move Lab: Offer to Market

6 weeks5–8 peopleFounding cohort

Take expertise that is already real and turn it into an offer you can explain, price, build and put in front of actual buyers.

For service providers, agency owners, consultants, coaches and independent experts. You don’t need another year of brand contemplation. You need a credible offer and enough support to test it.

The offer

Clarify the buyer, the problem, the promise, the scope and the price, so it’s something a buyer can actually say yes to.

The materials

Use AI to build the page, proposal, messages and sales assets you actually need, in days instead of months.

The rehearsal & the nervous-system work

Practise the buyer conversation, and notice what happens around visibility, asking and pricing. Design a stretch you can actually take.

The market

Get the offer in front of real people early. AI feedback doesn’t count as customer evidence.

The system

Weekly Next Move Briefs, an optional midweek nudge, and a 30-day continuation plan so the learning holds after the cohort ends.

Cadence

One 90-minute group session a week, plus two individual work reviews each across the six weeks. A capable paid AI account is required.

The outcome

Not guaranteed sales. A substantially better offer, real buyer evidence, usable materials, and a way of working that gets you into the market sooner.
