Between knowing and doing

The gap between knowing and doing.

  • You can understand that you should raise the price and still rewrite the proposal six times.
  • You can know you need to delegate and somehow keep becoming the bottleneck.
  • You can have a perfectly sensible content plan and publish nothing for a year.
I know that last one personally.

It isn’t automatically, “What’s wrong with my nervous system?” Sometimes the offer is weak, the plan unrealistic, or the system missing. And sometimes something internal is holding you back. That’s what we have to see.

Good work is figuring out which is which.
