THE GAP BETWEEN KNOWING AND DOING

Sometimes you need a better strategy.
Sometimes you need a different way into it.

Sending the proposal is different from knowing you should send it. So is holding the price, publishing something with your name on it, handing over control, or choosing one direction and letting the others go.

I don't assume every delay is a nervous-system problem. Sometimes the offer isn't good enough yet. Sometimes the workload is ridiculous. Often the business question and the human response are tangled together.

THE METHOD

Seven lenses.
One real business issue.

This is a coaching map, not seven compulsory weeks of preparation.

01Business RealityWhat is actually happening?

Start with the facts, the commercial goal and the decision. Sometimes the business itself needs fixing before we interpret anything psychologically.

02Relationship to the IssueWhat does this move mean to you?

Visibility, money, authority, rejection and responsibility can carry more meaning than the spreadsheet shows.

03Somatic MappingWhat happens as you approach it?

Notice the bodily response and protective impulse without treating it as a diagnosis or an instruction.

04Adaptive StrategyHow could the approach work better?

Change the strategy when it deserves changing. Use AI to explore, build and reduce unnecessary friction.

05Safety DesignWhat support makes the attempt workable?

Prepare the environment, information, rehearsal or support that lets you stay present enough to try.

06Strategic StretchWhat meaningful action will you take?

Not a shove. Not endless preparation. An edge that matters enough to teach us something.

07Integration into SystemsWhat happened, and what do we keep?

Review the result, recover, learn, and turn what worked into a repeatable part of the business.
