THE METHOD

Seven lenses.
One real business issue.

This is a coaching map, not seven compulsory weeks.

01Business RealityWhat is actually happening?

Start with the facts, commercial goal and decision.

02Relationship to the IssueWhat does this move mean to you?

Visibility, money, authority, rejection and responsibility can carry more meaning than the spreadsheet shows.

03Somatic MappingWhat happens as you approach it?

Notice the bodily response and protective impulse without treating it as a diagnosis.

04Adaptive StrategyHow could the approach work better?

Change the strategy when it deserves changing. Use AI to reduce unnecessary friction.

05Safety DesignWhat support makes the attempt workable?

Prepare the environment, information, rehearsal or support.

06Strategic StretchWhat meaningful action will you try?

Not a shove. Not endless preparation. An edge that matters enough to teach us something.

07Integration into SystemsWhat happened, and what will we keep?

Review, recover, learn and make what worked repeatable.
