Nervous system

What happens when the good strategy asks something of you: visibility, uncertainty, conflict, asking for money, letting go of control. We get curious about that too.

AI

Not as a gimmick. As a working partner. We use it to think, build, rehearse, analyse conversations and turn ideas into usable business work before another week disappears.

Between knowing and doing

The interesting bit is usually between knowing and doing.

  • You can understand that you should raise the price and still rewrite the proposal six times.
  • You can know you need to delegate and somehow keep becoming the bottleneck.
  • You can have a perfectly sensible content plan and publish nothing for a year.
I know that last one personally.

The question isn’t automatically, “What is wrong with my nervous system?” Sometimes the offer is weak. Sometimes the plan is unrealistic. Sometimes you need a better system. And sometimes the move carries more threat, meaning or history than the spreadsheet can see.

Good work is figuring out which is which.
